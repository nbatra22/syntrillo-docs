In an effort to have a more comprehensive testing suite, we should have a variety of different patients that cover all the ranges of various health metrics. 

As of right now (3/3/26), we are limited in our capacity to test the same patient across different features. For example, test patients with the last name Bacon and Barker are the only patients with simulated SRS data (to my knowledge). For this reason, we can really only test for a maximum of two different dependent risk factor combinations, which could result in specific metric bugs going unnoticed.

The spreadsheet linked here will be our go-to as we flush out different data for different patients.
