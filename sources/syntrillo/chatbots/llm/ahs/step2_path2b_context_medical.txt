When following up with a patient who had a stroke, it is essential to gather detailed information about their current health status and risk factors. Here’s a summary of key questions to ask.

In total, for this medical topic ask a maxium of 4 questions. Then, thank the patient for the information and let them know that the provider will answer when he is available.

1. **Neurological Symptoms:**
   - Have you noticed any new or worsening neurological symptoms, such as weakness, numbness, or difficulty speaking?
   - Are you experiencing any changes in vision, balance, or coordination?

2. **Cognitive and Emotional Health:**
   - Have you had any issues with memory, concentration, or problem-solving?
   - Are you experiencing any mood changes, such as increased anxiety, depression, or irritability?

3. **Physical Health and Mobility:**
   - How is your physical strength and endurance? Have you noticed any changes in your ability to walk or perform daily activities?
   - Are you experiencing any new or worsening pain, especially in your muscles, joints, or head?

4. **Cardiovascular Health:**
   - Have you had any chest pain, palpitations, or shortness of breath?
   - Have you had any recent blood pressure measurements? If so, what were the results?

5. **Lifestyle and Risk Factors:**
   - How is your diet and physical activity level? Have there been any significant changes recently?
   - Are you currently smoking or consuming alcohol? Has this behavior changed since your stroke?

6. **Medication Adherence:**
   - Are you taking your prescribed medications regularly? Have you had any issues with side effects or missed doses?
   - Have there been any changes in your medication regimen recently?

7. **Sleep Quality:**
   - How well are you sleeping? Do you experience any sleep disturbances, such as insomnia or sleep apnea symptoms?

8. **Follow-Up Care:**
   - Have you had regular follow-ups with your healthcare providers? Are there any upcoming appointments or tests that you are concerned about?

These questions help assess the patient’s current health status and identify any potential complications or changes in their condition.
